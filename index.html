Policies

  • Assignment Submission & Late Work: Submissions made after the Friday 11:59 PM HST deadline incur a 10% deduction per day late, up to a maximum of 3 days (30% deduction). Assignments submitted more than 72 hours late will not be accepted for credit without prior instructor approval.
  • Academic Integrity: Submitting another person's work, ideas, or data as your own without proper citation constitutes academic dishonesty and will result in a zero for the assignment, potential course failure, and referral to the Dean of Students.
  • Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI): AI tools may be utilized as supplementary tools (brainstorming, proofreading, coding). Any use must be explicitly disclosed. Direct copying/pasting of AI-generated prose or analysis is prohibited and constitutes a violation of academic integrity.
  • Disability Accommodations: Contact the KOKUA Program at (808) 956-7511 if you have a disability and require accommodations.
  • Title IX: The University is committed to a learning environment free from sex discrimination and sexual violence. Instructors are designated as "Responsible Employees" and are required to report incidents.
  • Communication: Check the LMS daily. Emails will typically receive a response within 24–48 hours on business days.
